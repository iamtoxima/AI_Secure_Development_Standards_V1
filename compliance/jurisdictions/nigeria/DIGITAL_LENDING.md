# Nigeria Digital Lending Baseline

Verified against FCCPC official sources: 2026-08-12

This module activates only when the project provides or materially facilitates
digital/electronic/non-traditional consumer lending in Nigeria.

## Current Baseline

FCCPC has issued the Digital, Electronic, Online or Non-Traditional (DEON)
Consumer Lending Regulations 2025 and related guidance.

Current FCCPC material emphasizes areas including:

- registration/compliance;
- fairness and transparency;
- responsible conduct;
- data privacy;
- accessible redress;
- ethical recovery;
- clear loan terms;
- responsible lending.

## AI Rule

Do not treat a generic fintech app, savings tool, marketplace, or payment app as
a digital lender merely because money is involved.

If lending/credit exists:

1. identify lender/intermediary roles;
2. identify whether credit is consumer, unsecured, digital/non-traditional;
3. verify current FCCPC applicability and registration requirements;
4. verify other relevant CBN/state/sector rules;
5. obtain qualified legal/compliance review before production.

## Engineering Controls

When applicable:

- do not scrape/abuse contacts or private device data for collection/recovery;
- clearly present loan terms and material charges;
- do not use deceptive pre-authorized/automatic lending behavior;
- enforce privacy/data-minimization controls;
- audit lending and recovery actions;
- provide complaint/redress paths;
- keep regulatory/product claims accurate.
