# Nigeria Privacy and Data Protection Baseline

Verified against Nigeria Data Protection Commission sources: 2026-08-12

Primary statutory baseline: Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDP Act).  
The NDPC also references the NDP Act General Application and Implementation
Directive (GAID) 2025 in current guidance.

## Applicability

NDPC guidance states that the NDP Act can apply where:

- an organization is domiciled/resident/operating in Nigeria;
- personal-data processing occurs in Nigeria; or
- an organization outside Nigeria processes personal data of a data subject in
  Nigeria.

Do not assume incorporation outside Nigeria automatically removes the project
from scope.

## Engineering Principles

For personal data:

- identify a lawful basis for processing;
- process fairly, lawfully, and transparently;
- collect for specified/legitimate purposes;
- minimize collection to what is necessary;
- keep data accurate where relevant;
- retain no longer than necessary for the applicable purpose/legal basis;
- apply appropriate security;
- document processors/providers and data flows;
- provide clear privacy information.

## Data Subject Rights

NDPC currently describes rights including:

- information;
- access;
- rectification;
- objection;
- restriction;
- data portability;
- erasure/right to be forgotten;
- complaint to the supervisory authority;
- rights relating to automated decision-making.

The exact workflow, exceptions, identity verification, and retention/legal-hold
rules require current legal/compliance verification.

## Product Change Rule

When the product begins collecting a new category of personal data or uses data
for a new purpose:

1. update the data inventory;
2. reassess lawful basis;
3. update privacy notice/disclosures;
4. update retention/deletion;
5. update app-store disclosures;
6. update processors/provider records;
7. evaluate whether consent or another legal step is required;
8. evaluate DPIA or other current NDPC obligations for high-risk processing.

## Governance

The project must not claim NDPC registration/audit/compliance status without
real organizational evidence.

Determine whether current requirements for Data Controllers/Processors of Major
Importance, registration, compliance audits, DPO/DPCO involvement, DPIAs, breach
reporting, or cross-border transfer apply to the specific organization.
